On [sketchnomad.app](https://sketchnomad.app), every sketch has a place. The curated historical layer adds **46 plein air and Impressionist masters** and **676 paintings**, each pinned to the coordinates where the work belongs — a harbor in Normandy, a wheat field in Provence, a street in Tokyo.

This guide lists who is in the collection, grouped by movement, with links to each artist profile on the map.

## Why put masterworks on a map?

*En plein air* — painting outdoors from direct observation — is inseparable from place. Light, weather, and geography shaped what Turner saw on the Thames, what Monet chased at Giverny, what Boudin recorded on the Normandy coast.

When a painting carries a map pin, you can:

- Jump to the **exact region** where an artist worked
- **Compare artists** who painted the same coast or river valley
- **Plan a sketch trip** with real artistic reference points
- Study technique **by place**, not just by museum wall label

Historical works sit on the same map as sketches uploaded by living artists — one continuous, place-based layer.

Open [sketchnomad.app](https://sketchnomad.app), then:

1. Tap **Curated Artists** to browse by name

## Frequently asked questions

### How many artists and paintings are included?

**46 artists** and **676 location-tagged works** as of August 2026. The catalog grows as new masters and paintings are researched and geotagged.

### Is it free to browse?

Yes — the map, curated artists, and historical pins are free at [sketchnomad.app](https://sketchnomad.app).

### Who is this collection for?

Anyone who works from observation outdoors: plein air painters, Impressionist landscape students, urban sketchers, travel painters, and watercolorists looking for place-based reference.

### Can I see where Monet or Van Gogh painted?

Yes. Each work has coordinates. Open [Monet](https://sketchnomad.app/artist/claude_monet) or [Van Gogh](https://sketchnomad.app/artist/vincent_vangogh) and explore their pins on the interactive map.

### How is this different from Google Arts & Culture?

Sketch Nomad is **map-first** — you start from geography, not a museum catalog. Living artists and historical masters share the same place-based layer, built for people who sketch and paint outdoors today.

### Will more artists be added?

Yes. Follow [@sketch.nomad on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/sketch.nomad/) for updates as the curated layer expands.
