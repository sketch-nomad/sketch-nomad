Sketch Nomad is made by a sketcher, for sketchers. It is a platform for urban sketchers, plein air artists and outdoor painters that puts work made outdoors on a map.

## Why I built it

Hi, I'm Mani-Nesan. I sketch outdoors, and for years I kept wishing I could see my art journey on a map.

My sketches were scattered across sketchbooks, and I had no way to remember where inspiration had struck. So I decided to build the tool I had always imagined. Six months later, the first version of Sketch Nomad was ready, and I have been improving it ever since.

I run it as a small, independent project, and I keep it that way on purpose: steady and reliable matters more to me than fast and flashy.

## What you can do

- Pin sketches to the place they were made and build a map of your own work.
- Explore historical paintings by artists who worked outdoors, shown where they were painted.
- Plan an open sketch session and invite artists nearby.

The web app is live at [sketchnomad.app](https://sketchnomad.app). It is free to use, and a free version will always exist. iOS and Android apps are planned.

## How your work is treated

Your artwork stays yours, and it is never used to train AI models. More in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
