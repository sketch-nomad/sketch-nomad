## Sharing and locations

Artist profiles are public. You can choose whether a sketch record is public or private. Public sketches and their location tags can be seen by other people, and may be copied or indexed by search engines.

A private sketch record is hidden from public listings. Image files currently use public links, so someone with a direct image link may still be able to view the image. Please do not upload sensitive images that need restricted access.

Take care when sharing exact locations, especially your home or places you visit regularly. Location tags and map information may not always be accurate. Respect local rules, property and other people when visiting a location.
